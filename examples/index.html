Phase 1 walking skeleton. Geometry comes from the built-in registry, served here out of examples/data by ApexMaps.setGeoSource('../geo/'). Run npm run data:examples first.

Choropleth, default configuration

No styling passed: Equal Earth projection, quantile classification, OkLab-sampled ramp, computed class breaks in the legend, and a no-data swatch. Click a legend class to mute it. Hover, scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to zoom in.

Bubble series: proportional symbols

The right mark for absolute magnitudes. Area is proportional to value (square-root radius scaling), largest painted first so small circles stay clickable, and the legend nests three reference circles so areas can actually be decoded. Zoom in: the circles hold their size because radius encodes a value.

Arc series: great-circle routes

Singapore's long-haul network, drawn as great circles. The routes to Los Angeles and Vancouver cross the Pacific, so they are cut at the antimeridian and re-enter on the left edge instead of streaking back across the map. The São Paulo route sweeps south past the Cape, which is genuinely the shortest path. Thin arcs carry an invisible wider hit path so they stay hoverable.

The geometry registry

Three packs, no geometry handling, no join configuration, no projection and no view. Each pack states its own recommended key, which is why "CA" and "DE-BY" just join; recommends how it is meant to be drawn, which is why the US gets Alaska inset and Europe is not lost inside a view that reaches Réunion; and carries its licence, so the Eurostat attribution renders without being asked for.
